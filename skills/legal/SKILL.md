# Legal Skill Pack v0.1

This pack contains low-risk legal document processing and source-verification skills.

## Scope

- Extract and normalize information from user-provided text.
- Search the configured private Retrieval Gateway.
- Preserve source anchors where the input provides them.
- Return warnings when the knowledge base is unavailable or an extraction is heuristic.

## Non-goals

- No final legal advice or legal conclusion.
- No claim that a citation is authoritative merely because it was parsed.
- No automatic determination of liability, crime, enforceability, or litigation outcome.

## Review boundary

Outputs involving legal applicability, deadlines, legal characterization, or conflicting sources must be reviewed by a qualified human. Citation verification is only a retrieval match and is not a substitute for source validation.
